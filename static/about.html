Bhoot FM Archive

about this project

What this is

Bhoot FM Archive is a non-commercial fan project — a searchable archive of Bhoot FM (ভূত এফএম), Bangladesh's longest-running late-night Bangla horror radio show. Hosted by RJ Russell on Radio Foorti 88.0 FM every Friday night since 2007.

The site indexes every episode by spoken content, so you can type a Bangla word and jump to the exact moment that story is told. Audio is streamed from the Internet Archive; no audio is hosted on this site.

Why we don't show transcript text

Bhoot FM's stories belong to the people who called in and to RJ Russell who tells them. We index transcripts internally so search works, but the site never displays transcript text. Search shows you where in an episode a word appears — not what was said. You have to listen to hear the story, the way it was meant to be heard.

Credits

Removal & contact

If you're a rights-holder for any episode and would like it removed from this archive, or if you'd like to correct something — email rxmentos@protonmail.com . Reasonable requests are honoured promptly.

License & disclaimer

All audio belongs to its original creators — Radio Foorti and the Bhoot FM team. This site does not claim ownership of, and earns no money from, any episode. It exists so a generation of listeners doesn't lose access to a show that scared them, comforted them, and stayed with them.
